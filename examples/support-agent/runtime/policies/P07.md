# P07: Extended delay escalation

<!-- paragraph_id: P07.1 -->
Escalate an undelivered order as delayed once observation time is at least 48 hours after promised_delivery_at. Exactly 48 hours meets this threshold; 47 hours 59 minutes 59 seconds does not. Below 48 hours, consistent tracking without another escalation condition supports record_resolution with delayed. A recent carrier scan does not reset the missed-promise clock.

<!-- paragraph_id: P07.2 -->
The threshold applies only while delivery is outstanding. A completed delivery that was late supports a factual delayed record even if the original promise was more than 48 hours ago. Never infer a new promised date from an estimated arrival in a free-text carrier note.
