# P02: Promised delivery time and fulfillment

<!-- paragraph_id: P02.1 -->
Compare instants in UTC at the registered business observation time. For an undelivered order, a missed promise starts strictly after promised_delivery_at; equality is not late. For a delivered order, compare its valid delivered timestamp with promised_delivery_at, not with observation time. Delivery at the promise boundary is on_time; delivery after it is delayed.

<!-- paragraph_id: P02.2 -->
Complete, consistent evidence without a dispute or escalation condition supports record_resolution: on_time when delivery did not miss its promise, otherwise delayed. On_time for an order still in transit means not overdue at observation time, never that delivery is complete. A delivered label without any delivery event is missing evidence, not proof of receipt.
