# P03: Missing facts and clarification

<!-- paragraph_id: P03.1 -->
Use insufficient with request_information when a decision needs a missing related order, a missing delivery association, usable tracking events, a delivery event timestamp, or a specific description of the reported problem. Identify the missing relationship or clarification precisely. Do not invent facts, read another tenant's similarly named object, or assume delivered from an aggregate label without a delivery event.

<!-- paragraph_id: P03.2 -->
A vague report such as 'something is wrong' requires a description even when tracking looks normal. Missing evidence differs from contradictory evidence: absence alone is not conflicting. A positive dispute, an active critical carrier exception, or an established contradiction does not need to wait for unrelated missing information before escalation.
